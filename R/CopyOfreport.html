To better estimate standardized scores across age, we adopted gamlss and a Beta Binomial family to account for the ordinal nature of the data and the bounds.

dat_qstP = dat[!is.na(dat$QSTp_Total_parent),c("Code","Age","QSTp_Total_parent")]

fit_QSTp <- gamlss(
  cbind(QSTp_Total_parent, 30 - QSTp_Total_parent) ~ pbm(Age),
  sigma.fo = ~ pbm(Age),         
  family = BB,#BEINF,
  data   = dat_qstP
)
